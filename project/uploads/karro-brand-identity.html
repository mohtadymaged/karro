01 · The Name

Karro — the original marketplace on wheels

Before malls and apps, Egypt's marketplace was the karro — the vendor's cart that rolled through every street. It's commerce, nostalgia, and fun in one word, and it means the name and the logo tell the same story.

karro
كارو

Why it works: Every Egyptian knows the karro; every foreign resident learns it in their first month. It's warm and instantly commercial without being corporate — exactly "simple and fun." Two syllables, works in both scripts, easy to say in any accent. And because the karro roams the neighbourhood, it naturally carries the hyper-local promise: the market comes to you.

02 · The Mark

The cart, reinvented for Gouna

A friendly, rounded cart — unmistakably "marketplace" at any size. Inside the basket runs the lagoon wave that connects the community; on the handle flies a coral pennant, the vendor's flag that says "open for business."

Anatomy & clear space. Four elements: the coral pennant (always on the handle, always pointing right), the cart basket carrying the lagoon wave, and two wheels in Deep Water. Clear space equals one wheel diameter on all sides. Minimum size: 24 px digital, 8 mm print — below that, drop the wave and pennant and keep the cart silhouette.

Don'tTurn it into a generic e-commerce trolley — the pennant and wave are what make it Karro
Don'tFlip the mark — the cart always rolls to the right, toward the buyer
Don'tFill the basket with products or icons — the wave is the only content
Don'tRecolor the pennant — coral is its only color on lagoon and deep backgrounds

05 · Voice & Tone

Like a good neighbour, not a corporation

Karro talks the way Gouna talks: relaxed, direct, a little playful — and dead serious about trust when it matters.

Neighbourly

Short sentences, first names, zero jargon. We say "pickup" not "logistics coordination."

Sara is 5 minutes away. Want to meet at the marina?

Playful, not silly

One light touch per screen — an emoji, a wink in a success message. Never in trust or safety copy.

Sold! 🎉 Your karro rolls fast.

Straight about trust

Safety copy drops the playfulness completely. Plain, calm, specific.

Only verified Gouna residents can see your listing and your contact details.
